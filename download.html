Instructions for a Standard (Unlocked) Phone

Step 1: Download the App

Click the download button at the bottom of this page. Your phone will warn you that this type of file can harm your device. Tap "OK" or "Download Anyway".

Step 2: Open Your Files

Once the download finishes, pull down your notification shade and tap the downloaded file (it will be called app-debug.apk or PeacemakerV1.apk). If you can't find it there, open your "My Files" or "Files" app and look in the Downloads folder.

Step 3: Allow Installation

Your phone will likely say: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

Tap "Settings" on that popup, and toggle on "Allow from this source". Then tap the back arrow and try installing the file again.

Step 4: Install and Open

Tap "Install". Once it finishes, you can tap "Open" and begin using The Peacemaker.
