Instructions for Installation

Step 1: Download the Attachment

Go to the email you received from C43P Ministries. Tap the attachment (it will be named app-debug.apk or PeacemakerV1.apk) to download it to your phone.

Step 2: Open Your Files

Once the download finishes, pull down your notification shade and tap the downloaded file. If you can't find it there, open your "My Files" or "Files" app and look in the Downloads folder.

Step 3: Allow Installation

Your phone will likely say: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

Tap "Settings" on that popup, and toggle on "Allow from this source". Then tap the back arrow and try installing the file again.

Step 4: Install and Open

Tap "Install". Once it finishes, you can tap "Open" and begin using The Peacemaker.

Using a Work Phone or Locked Device?
