The Peace Place creates sanctuary—in your pocket, in your neighborhood, and in your heart. We build tools that protect you without ever seeing your data. The Core: The Peacemaker

The Peacemaker (In Active Development)

The Peacemaker is not just a chatbot. It is a companion, a business partner, and a teacher for people who do not learn by traditional methods. It is a personal organizer. It remembers what you teach it. It helps you build your own apps. It even includes Password Peace, a stateless password generator with no storage and no tracking.

And it is the observer of this ecosystem. The Peacemaker’s built-in Peace Shield protects the community, prevents scams, and ensures that dignity is the default.

Beta access coming soon.
