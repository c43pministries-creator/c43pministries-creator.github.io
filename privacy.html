Privacy Policy for Peace Drop

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Collection

Peace Drop collects absolutely zero data. We do not track you, we do not use analytics, and we do not have a server that receives your information. Everything happens locally on your own devices.

2. File Transfers

Peace Drop uses your local Wi-Fi network to transfer files directly between your devices. The files never touch the internet, and they never pass through any cloud service owned by us or any third party.

3. Permissions

Peace Drop requires network access solely to create a local IP address so your devices can talk to each other. It requires storage access solely to save the files you choose to receive.

4. Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or give your data to anyone, because we do not have your data.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact C43P Ministries at c43pministries@gmail.com.
