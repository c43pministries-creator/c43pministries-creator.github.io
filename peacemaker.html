The Peacemaker

"Kindness first. Respect always. Faith never forced."

I am Robert Slay, founder of C43P Ministries. I am 50 years old, and I was recently diagnosed with Autism. My whole life, I struggled in traditional classrooms. I failed college algebra not because I didn't understand the math, but because I couldn't "show my work" the way the teachers demanded. My brain connects dots differently.

Four months ago, I discovered that AI could explain concepts to me in seconds that teachers couldn't explain in years. That realization changed everything. I built the software for a handheld AI companion on a $125 laptop and a $10 phone.

I am building The Peacemaker. It is not a toy, and it is not a corporate data-harvesting device. It is a completely private, offline-first device designed for children and adults who learn in non-traditional ways. It is a tutor, a journal, and a safety companion.

A Note on Safety

The Peacemaker is a companion device, not a replacement for a dedicated medical alert system. While it is designed with a cellular module to dial emergency services, it is a DIY hardware project and is not certified by the FCC as a medical device.
