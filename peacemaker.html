"Kindness first. Respect always. Faith never forced."

I am Robert Slay, founder of C43P Ministries. I am 50 years old, and I was recently diagnosed with Autism. My whole life, I struggled in traditional classrooms. I failed college algebra not because I didn't understand the math, but because I couldn't "show my work" the way the teachers demanded. My brain connects dots differently.

A year ago, I discovered that AI could explain concepts to me in seconds that teachers couldn't explain in years. That realization changed everything. I built the software for a handheld AI companion on a $125 laptop and a $50 phone my younger brother bought for me, while navigating homelessness and protecting my boundaries from people trying to take advantage of my kindness.

I am not a corporation. I am one person. I need to get on my feet before I can start buying the physical devices to integrate, and eventually, connect those handheld devices to a satellite network. But right now, the digital foundation has to be built. I am asking for help so I can help people more than most can begin to understand.
